Process improvement demonstration

Cleaner applications. Faster reviews. Human-controlled decisions.

This interactive prototype demonstrates how front-end validation, OCR-assisted comparison, historical-data reuse, rules-based routing, and one consolidated officer screen could reduce repetitive work.

1. Capture once Application and OCR information enter one structured record.
2. Validate automatically Reference, history, confidence, and mismatch checks run immediately.
3. Route by exception Green, Amber, and Red queues focus effort according to complexity.
4. Preserve human authority Authorised officers retain control over final decisions.
Applicant side

Prevent avoidable errors

Searchable institution data, document-quality guidance, simulated OCR, historical prefill, and field-specific assistance.

Rules and routing

Prepare the case automatically

Applications are checked and placed into Green, Amber, or Red queues with reason codes and required actions.

Officer side

Review everything in one place

Applicant entries, OCR results, history, validation checks, decisions, timestamps, and audit records appear together.
